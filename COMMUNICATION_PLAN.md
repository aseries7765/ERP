# Communication Plan

## Channels
- **Internal**: dedicated Slack channel per incident/launch event.
- **External, routine**: status page (see STATUS_PAGE.md).
- **External, incident**: status page update + direct customer email
  for material impact, per SLA_DEFINITIONS.md.
- **Regulatory**: per docs/security/INCIDENT_RESPONSE.md — GDPR 72h,
  HIPAA per its own timeline, coordinated with legal.

## Templates (to be drafted before launch, not included here since
tone/branding is a product/marketing decision outside this module's
scope)
- Launch announcement
- Incident acknowledgment ("we're aware, investigating")
- Incident resolution
- Post-incident summary for affected customers

## Cadence during an active incident
Status page updates at least every 30 minutes during an active
CRITICAL/HIGH incident, even if the update is "still investigating" —
silence during an incident erodes trust more than an honest "no new
information yet."
